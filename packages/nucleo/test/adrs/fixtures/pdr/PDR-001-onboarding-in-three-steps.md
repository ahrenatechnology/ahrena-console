# PDR-001: Onboarding in three steps

## Context and Problem Statement

New accounts drop at the fifth onboarding screen.

## Considered Options

* Three steps, the rest deferred
* Keep the five steps with a progress bar

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "Three steps, the rest deferred", because it is the only one the test cohort finished.

### Consequences

* Good, because completion rose in the test cohort.
* Bad, because billing details move to the first invoice.
